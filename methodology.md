# Research Methodology

## City: Hyderabad | Segment: Complex APIs & Regulated Pharma

## Sources Used
1. PHARMEXCIL USFDA-registered site list
2. WHO prequalification inspection database
3. Genome Valley / Bio-Pharma SEZ tenant lists
4. LinkedIn Sales Navigator (pharma mfg, Hyderabad, 50-500 employees)
5. Screener.in / Tofler for revenue verification
6. PharmAcompass USDMF filing tracker
7. BioSpectrum India news archive
8. VCCircle for PE/funding checks
9. MCA/Tofler for ownership structure
10. Company websites + LinkedIn for C4 (DM background)

## Research Process
- Step 1: Built raw universe of ~85 Hyderabad pharma manufacturers
- Step 2: Applied auto-disqualifiers (revenue >500Cr, PE-owned, service companies)
- Step 3: Scored remaining ~45 companies on all 6 Federer criteria
- Step 4: Selected 23 PASS companies + documented 12 FAIL companies
- Step 5: Verified all A-band companies against primary sources

## Key Learnings
- Revenue changes fast: Natco, Neuland both crossed Rs.500Cr recently
- PE investment is instant disqualifier: Fleming (InvAscent Dec 2023)
- CRO ≠ Manufacturer: many Genome Valley companies sell services not products
- Genome Valley = highest C6 signal density in Hyderabad
- DMF filing velocity (PharmAcompass) is a strong verifiable C6 signal
